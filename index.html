📚 How to Use LaunchLedger
LaunchLedger is a startup budget tracker designed to help you plan, track, and manage your business expenses — from your first legal filing to your first marketing campaign. Follow the steps below to get the most out of every feature.
1. Getting Started
1
Register: Create an account with your name and email. You'll receive a unique Access Code — save it! This code + your email is how you log back in from any device.
2
Choose your role: Select Admin (full access, can delete tasks & invite a user) or User (shared access, needs a business code from an Admin).
3
Set up Business Info: Go to the Business Info page and fill in your business name, industry, stage, budget, and funding sources. This drives all your charts and projections.
2. Dashboard
Your dashboard shows four key metrics at a glance: Total Estimated spend, Actual Spent, Remaining Budget, and Tasks Complete. Below are four charts: budget allocation by category (donut), spend timeline by month (bar), spending by priority, and task completion status. Alerts appear automatically if you're over budget or have overdue tasks.
3. Task Board
1
Financial Tasks have estimated and actual costs. Click "+ Financial Task" to add one. Fill in the expense name, category, priority, cost, due date, and vendor. Once a Financial Task is marked Done, a "Create RunBook" button appears on its card.
2
Non-Financial Tasks are action items with no cost — like setting up social media, writing your business vision, or doing market research. Click "+ Non-Financial Task" to browse the pre-built library and choose which ones to add to your board.
3
Cards move between To Do, In Progress, and Done columns by editing the task and changing the Status dropdown.
4
Vendor fields: On each Financial Task you can select a vendor from the dropdown (auto-fills website and description), or type in any vendor manually. You can also add a Contact Person, email, and phone number — the vendor website becomes a clickable link.
4. Budget Planner
A full table view of all your expenses. Filter by category or status. Estimated and actual totals are calculated automatically at the bottom. Admins can delete rows; Users can edit but not delete.
5. Cash Flow
A 12-month projection showing your cash balance over time vs. monthly expenses. Set your Monthly Revenue and Total Funding in Business Info to make the projection accurate. The burn rate table below the chart breaks it down month by month.
6. RunBook
After a Financial Task is marked Done, click "Create RunBook" on its card. This opens a form where you log vendor details: contact info, contract dates, payment terms, renewal dates, contract value, performance rating, and notes. All RunBook entries appear on the RunBook page for easy reference.
Payment Terms (GAAP Standard)
Net 7/15/30/60/90 — Payment is due within that many days of invoice. Due on Receipt — Pay immediately upon receiving the invoice. 2/10 Net 30 — Take a 2% discount if you pay within 10 days; otherwise full amount due in 30. COD — Cash on Delivery. Prepaid — Payment required before service begins.
7. Funding Overview
In Business Info, you can select multiple funding sources (Self-Funded, Angel Investment, SBA Loan, etc.) and enter the dollar amount for each. The Total Funding field auto-calculates the grand total. This figure feeds into your Cash Flow projections and runway calculation.
8. Reports
View estimated vs. actual spend by category (horizontal bar chart), a Budget Health Score (progress bars showing how much of each category budget you've used), and a summary statistics panel with completion rates, highest categories, and variance analysis.
